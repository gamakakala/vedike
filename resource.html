Gamaka, also known as Kaavyavaachana, is a traditional art form that combines poetic singing with eloquent explanation. A pair of performers collaborate—one sings verses emphasizing mood and narrative; the other interprets and elaborates. Most performances draw from classical Kannada and Sanskrit epics.
